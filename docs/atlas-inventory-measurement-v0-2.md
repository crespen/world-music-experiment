# Atlas inventory measurement — Africa and North America v0.2

**Date:** November 12, 2026 · **Method:** count Markdown list items in the numbered geographic chapters of the two revised Earth to Ear research manuscripts.

| Manuscript | Geographic range counted | Numbered-section bullet entries |
|---|---|---:|
| African Musical Atlas v0.2 | Sections 01–05, African Union regions | 711 |
| North American Musical Atlas v0.2 | Sections 01–29, country/territory and cross-border Indigenous headings | 332 |
| **Combined** | | **1,043** |

**These are NOT 1,043 distinct validated genres.** A single bullet may name several styles, instruments, genres or musical forms; some entries are overlapping or shared across borders. The category of each term still needs normalized type (genre, tradition, practice, technique, instrument, scene, composition) and rigorous deduplication.

For historical comparison, v0.1 contained **827 African candidate labels** (680 country-related and 147 supplemental) and **731 North American candidate entries**, totaling **1,558 raw research listings** under an older and more microgenre-heavy method. That total is not directly comparable to v0.2.

The platform should report separately:
1. **Research inventory entries** — how many candidate bullets/terms are documented.
2. **Verified distinct musical entities** — deduplicated, type-resolved, evidence-backed.
3. **Public music-first listening recordings** — specific performances with appropriate source and playback labels.
4. **Historical/cultural context sources** — documentaries, scholarly articles, and interviews.

Do not present research inventory counts as verified genre counts or music library recording counts. No cultural quotas or implicit ranking follow from these quantities.

## Anonymous design insight
A first-time visitor described finding the platform intriguing while wanting clearer visitor benefits and more orientation around performers, instruments, and historical pathways. In response, the homepage now explains public listening benefits and the distinction between public discovery and private contributor journals; generated recording pages foreground the musical source and make further context optional.
