**Snapshot:** November 12, 2026 · **67 active candidate recordings**. The entries below are an exact source-link inventory captured from the live catalog, **not a live-playback audit**. **How to review:** Open the source link and note whether it is the intended recording; whether music begins in the first ~30 seconds (or a deep link is needed); whether a substantial song or performance continues largely uninterrupted; and whether the source is suitable as a primary listening assignment.
