# Earth to Ear — Podcast Topic & Guest Prospect Catalog

**Initial working catalog · November 11, 2026**  
**Status:** Concept and research. **No guests have been invited, booked or confirmed.** A cited biography establishes topical fit, not availability, interest or willingness to participate.  
**Working series name:** *Earth to Ear: Conversations in Listening* (not a final brand or public launch announcement).

## Editorial premise

An audio companion to the open listening library: **listen, notice, converse, and discover**. Hosts and guests need not agree about what a recording evokes. The show can be curious, warm, provocative and funny without belittling artists or communities.

It should be intelligible to a person who knows little about a musical style, rewarding to someone who knows it well, and grounded in recordings listeners can find at their original sources. A personal heritage connection can be an invitation, never a deduction from ancestry DNA or a claim that an artist speaks for a genetic lineage.

## Conversational format & co-host

Inspired by the **division of roles** on Neil deGrasse Tyson's *StarTalk*, which pairs a subject-matter host with comedians and specialists (official: https://startalkmedia.com/about-us).

**Founder / lead host:** Introduces the question, connects the music to the larger Earth to Ear experiment, draws out insights and holds the intellectual thread.  
**Comic / curious-listener co-host:** Brings spontaneity, asks questions a listener might not ask, notices contradictions, playfully challenges jargon and helps keep the conversation human. Their job is to *listen*, not to compete with the musical guest.  
**Episode guest:** Artist, tradition-bearer, scholar, archivist, producer, or music journalist with direct knowledge; for community-specific material, prioritize authentic practitioner voices.

**Tone boundaries:** Humor about our assumptions, pretensions, misunderstandings and unexpected discoveries is welcome. Not at the expense of culture, language, accent, spirituality or a guest's background. Correct misstatements without embarrassment. No faux expertise.

**Casting, not appointment:** Seek someone conversationally generous, culturally curious, musically open-minded, funny without interrupting, comfortable acknowledging ignorance, and able to share airtime. Try **two or three recorded, unpublished chemistry sessions** with prospective partners before making a permanent co-host arrangement. A gifted improviser or broadcaster may fit better than a high-profile stand-up comedian. Rotating comic guests are a valid pilot alternative.

**Provisional episode shape** (flexible): 25–40 minutes, with a brief opening question, 2–3 listening moments discussed (not assumed to be licensed clips), guest conversation, and one optional listener prompt. A companion webpage links to original recordings and sources. Prefer founder + co-host + one guest; do not crowd every episode.

**Music rights:** Link listeners to recordings at original sources by default. Do not insert copyrighted recordings in the show on an assumption that short clips are automatically fair use. Obtain required recording/composition permissions or use explicitly licensed / independently owned audio; confirm permissions before production.

**Chemistry test:** Offer a short unfamiliar recording; ask the would-be co-host what caught their attention, how they would help an audience hear it differently, and what they would avoid assuming. Record off-air; assess rapport, interruptions, pacing and respect. No requirement that the person be famous.

## Podcast-to-site connection

Every published episode should link to a companion **Listening Journey** or set of source recordings, an optional reflection prompt, guest/source credits, and one suggested next listening experience. In turn, relevant catalog cards and articles can point to the episode. Do not introduce a paywall, forced registration, ratings game or podcast-only listening format.

## Workflow

- Add topics as **Idea → Research → Recording anchors verified → Guest prospect → Outreach → Scheduled → Recorded → Published**.
- Named guests progress separately through **Not contacted → Researching introduction → Contacted → In discussion → Confirmed / Declined**. A topic can develop without securing the preferred guest.
- Track only essential fields: date added, place/style, question, 2–3 specific recording candidates, guest type, sources, status and next research step.
- **No automatic invitations** or announcements, no fictional episode booking, and no need to commission every style or every continent.
- First pilot recommendation: **P-003 (Ghana guitar-band highlife)** or **P-002 (jazz vocal phrasing)**; test the comedic co-host format in a private pilot before publishing.
