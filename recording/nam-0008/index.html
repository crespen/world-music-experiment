Meet the recording

A Havana musician and band combine Afro-Cuban vocal approaches and rhythms with funk and soul. Listen for groove changes and exchanges between instruments and voices.

What might you notice?

Listen for a sound or moment that catches your attention—a rhythm, a voice, an instrument, a change in texture, or something unexpected. You do not need technical terms or a correct answer.
