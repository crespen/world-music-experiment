**Release date:** November 11, 2026

## Public identity
- Earth to Ear
- **A Global Listening Experiment**

## Listener catalog
- Twelve known preview-limited or overly short sources were replaced by twelve distinct long-form or full-recording selections, while preserving historical originals as inactive database records.

- Full-duration browser playback and regional availability are still a QA requirement; no provider access is guaranteed solely by editorial research.

## Standing policy
See `docs/listening-access-curation-standard.md` and `docs/daily-music-discovery-standard.md`. Fully accessible, legitimate recordings are now the default; 30-second subscription-only preview pages are no longer accepted as normal listening selections.
