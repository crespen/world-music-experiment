The Jazz Voice

From blues phrasing to a new generation of interpreters

A journey through ways of telling a story in song—swinging a phrase, stretching a melody, leaving space, or treating a standard as something personal. This is a selective vocal history, not a claim that blues and jazz are identical or that one singer simply replaces the next.

LISTENING ARC · JAMAICA

Jamaican Reggae

Rhythm, historical memory and renewal

This arc begins at the emergence of reggae and follows a roots-oriented thread through new kinds of rhythm-section playing and contemporary revival. Ska and rocksteady are essential earlier contexts; dub, lovers rock and dancehall are important branches, not failures to follow one straight path.

01

1968 · Reggae takes its name

A sharper rhythmic identity

Toots & The Maytals' lively performance helps reveal how a new Jamaican sound came into focus after ska and rocksteady.

LISTEN AT THE ORIGINAL SOURCEDo the Reggay (1968 recording)

Toots & The Maytals · Jamaica

Hear this recording ↗Find in library →
About the artist

Singer Frederick “Toots” Hibbert and the Maytals bridged multiple eras of Jamaican popular music.

1973 · The international anthem

Hear this recording ↗Find in library →
