Why This Experiment Exists

Earth to Ear is an invitation to listen before judgment, to approach music with curiosity over expertise, and to discover the richness of musical traditions across cultures. By listening together, we can begin to hear patterns of response and meaning—what moves us, what feels familiar or new, and how music speaks across different lives and places.

The public listening library is open to all, offering recordings from around the world for anyone to explore. Invited contributors can also share personal reflections, which add depth and diverse perspectives to the experiment, helping us understand not just the music, but how and why it resonates.

A world of music. A world of ways to listen.

A performance may be familiar to an entire community and new only to the person encountering it for the first time. Every tradition has its own history, knowledge, practitioners and value independent of our project. We will not measure the world's music against a single cultural standard or confuse our personal reactions with researched historical claims. We are beginning with Africa, then extending the library across the globe, looking for musical connections without assuming they must exist.

The broader experiment may one day trace documented musical crossroads and explore new creative possibilities in a Musical Laboratory. For now, listening comes first, and curiosity is enough.
