Meet the recording

This UNESCO documentary introduces Gnawa music and its sacred and social settings. Attend to the repeated pulse, metallic percussion, string timbre and communal vocals without assuming the film documents an unrestricted private ritual.

What might you notice?

Listen for a sound or moment that catches your attention—a rhythm, a voice, an instrument, a change in texture, or something unexpected. You do not need technical terms or a correct answer.
