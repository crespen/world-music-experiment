# Earth to Ear — 43-recording global expansion, November 12, 2026

**Status:** Approved active Supabase catalog selections; public recording pages and sitemap generated in the repository. Third-party source playback was verified by source descriptions and published pages, **not by a complete audio/video play-through in every region**.

The release expands the catalog from 71 to **114 active recordings**. It includes Randy Weston, John Coltrane, Thelonious Monk, Oscar Peterson, Mary Lou Williams, Sister Rosetta Tharpe, Mingus Big Band, and Pharoah Sanders. The jazz selections distinguish original historical audio from later visual productions and tribute performances.

## The selection rules

- Each item has a distinct musical focus, source, contributor/ensemble attribution as far as established, country/region, and an original listening prompt.
- This is a **catalog of source-linked performances**, not hosted recordings, copied audio, copyrighted image material, or lyrics.
- Links are playable at their original providers, subject to region and provider conditions. Some UNESCO videos are **heritage documentaries containing performance excerpts**, not uninterrupted performances.
- Sacred, community-restricted, or undocumented performances are **not assumed available** merely because a link exists. Sources here were publicly released by the institutions or creators noted.
- For later research, these musical signatures are observational starting points. They do not prove historical transmission, shared ancestry, or causal links.
- **Further verification:** Human listening reviews should inspect the amount of narration, music-start timestamps, misleading titles and dead links. Replace poor-fit documentary choices with music-first performances when genuinely available.

## Publication and maintenance

The public Supabase catalog is the source of truth. All 114 pages are produced by `scripts/generate-recording-pages.mjs` and the sitemap includes **126 URLs** (114 records plus 12 public section routes). This GitHub commit includes the 43 new generated HTML pages and sitemap so branch-based Pages remains consistent with the active catalog until the repository's Pages source is switched to GitHub Actions. The Actions workflow also rebuilds from Supabase and deploys the full static site.

## What remains

The November 12 shortlist contained 150 proposed research targets. **43 have been published**; a candidate does not count as published simply because it appears in the research slate. Continue source verification in musical-signature-diverse batches; prioritize underrepresented Indigenous traditions, historical recordings, music-first links, and continents still in starter status. Do not claim that the 25-per-continent additions goal is complete.
