# Earth to Ear — Open Continents & Continuous Catalog Growth
Adopted November 11, 2026. **Standing founder decision.**

## Governing rule
**All six geographic continent collections remain open for browsing and are never locked for lack of a minimum number of recordings.** Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, South America, and Oceania are navigation options, not completed collections. A continent may have one recording—or even temporarily none—without being labelled unavailable. Growth is continuous.

**Discovery should trigger catalog expansion, not await an arbitrary quota or editorial milestone.** Every recording expands listeners' possibilities without claiming to represent an entire culture, nation, genre or continent. There is no target number of recordings, number of countries, or scholarly threshold that must be reached before opening a collection.

## What makes a recording worthwhile?
A musical performance that offers a listening opportunity from a reasonably accessible original source.

Practical checks, and only these:
1. Identify the recording or particular performance and name performers where known.
2. Link to a publicly offered source providing meaningful listening (preferably 30+ free seconds); don't upload, rehost, or falsely guarantee its availability.
3. Describe in ordinary language what listeners might discover, including unfamiliar approaches **or** contemporary originality within familiar popular genres (rap, R&B, pop, country, rock, electronic music, etc.).
4. Don't publish material known to be private, restricted or culturally inappropriate for public sharing.

No requirement that the music be revolutionary, old, traditional, rare, academic, from an unrepresented nation, or a perfect fit with a quota.

## Operating workflow
- Daily discovery searches **all six continents**, not locked versus unlocked. Rotate sources and musical practices.
- Maintain a modest candidate queue and avoid duplicates. Strong source-linked candidates may be entered as individual public catalog records and journal IDs without waiting for other candidates to arrive.
- The founder retains editorial control of what is *published*; daily discovery briefs do not automatically modify the live catalog. The founder's explicit instruction to publish qualifies as approval. Do not create a cumbersome second approval bureaucracy.
- Homepage feature rotation remains separate from library publication: new tracks appear in their collections immediately; curated homepage selections respect their 30-day protection unless explicitly overridden.
- Keep traditional and contemporary music, historic innovations, and unexpectedly reimagined popular genres in the search scope. Seek diverse *listening experiences*, not mere national checkboxes.
- Maintain only lightweight link maintenance. Do not hold back the site for infallible playback verification or formal musicological completeness.

## Site implementation requirement
The main Explore Music continent tiles must always lead to working canonical routes:
`/africa/`, `/asia/`, `/europe/`, `/north-america/`, `/south-america/`, `/oceania/`.

Each page loads the active catalog for its continent, filters and displays the individual recording cards, and preserves links to original sources and the private contributor journal. Pages should not imply a continent is closed, completed or culturally represented by its current contents. Accessible collection count may be zero. Make descriptions welcoming, plain and temporary by design.

## Implementation decision — November 2026
The original 42 recordings cover Africa (31) and North America (11). Open the four remaining continents using available, source-linked opening performances; expand them continuously thereafter. This is not a launch ceremony or a finished survey.
